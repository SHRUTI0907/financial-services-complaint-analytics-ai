# Stakeholder Matrix

| Stakeholder | Interest | Influence | Primary Question |
|---|---:|---:|---|

| Chief Data/AI Officer | High | High | Which AI opportunities are valuable and governable? |
| CIO | Medium | High | What data, integration, and monitoring architecture is required? |
| Risk | High | High | Which themes indicate emerging consumer or operational risk? |
| Legal / Compliance | High | High | What controls are needed before AI touches customer-impacting workflows? |
| Business Unit Leaders | High | Medium | Which product lines need management attention? |

| Data Science | Medium | Medium | Which models are useful, measurable, and explainable? |
| End Users | Medium | Low | Will AI support work quality without removing human judgment? |
