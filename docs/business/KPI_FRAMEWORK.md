# KPI Framework

## Consumer-Risk KPIs
- Monthly complaint volume.
- Issue and product growth rate.
- Spike z-score versus trailing baseline.
- Narrative availability share.
- Repeat issue concentration.

## Operational KPIs
- Complaint volume by product, issue, company, channel, and state.
- Timely response rate.
- Response-category mix.
- Estimated observed handling workload.
- AI-addressable workload share.

## Financial KPIs
- Estimated annual capacity value.
- Annual net value.
- 3-year net value.
- ROI.
- NPV.
- Payback period.

## Model KPIs
- Baseline macro F1.
- Model macro F1.
- Class-level precision, recall, and F1.
- Retrieval citation coverage.
- Evidence-search citation check pass rate.

## Governance KPIs
- High-risk AI use cases.
- Required controls by use case.
- Human-review requirements.
- Monitoring requirements.
- Escalation-rule coverage.
