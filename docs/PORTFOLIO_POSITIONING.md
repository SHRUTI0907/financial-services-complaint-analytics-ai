# Portfolio Positioning

## One-Sentence Pitch
I built a real-data financial-services complaint analysis product that turns CFPB records into operational-risk signals, NLP themes, routing-model evidence, automation opportunities, value scenarios, and governance controls.

## The Role Fit
This project is aimed at Business Analyst, Data Analyst, Business Analytics, AI Business Analyst, Product Analytics, and AI Transformation roles.

It shows:
- business problem framing;
- public-data ingestion;
- data-quality checks;
- trend and anomaly analysis;
- NLP on real complaint narratives;
- baseline versus model evaluation;
- value modeling with assumptions;
- governance and risk controls;
- communication through an app and docs.

## The Human Angle
The strongest story is not “I built an AI app.”

The stronger story is:

I took a messy public operating dataset, asked what a financial-services team would actually need to know, and built a product that connects complaint patterns to decisions. The AI parts are there because the data has real text-heavy workflow problems. They are not decoration.

## What To Emphasize In Applications
- Real CFPB data, not synthetic use cases.
- Measured model improvement.
- Clear caveats around complaint data.
- Separation between observed evidence and proposed intervention.
- Human-in-the-loop AI design.
- Governance built into the product, not added at the end.

## What Not To Overclaim
- Do not say it saved money.
- Do not say the CFPB data proves company quality.
- Do not say the project is compliant with NIST.
- Do not say SEC/BLS enrichment is complete unless you run those scripts successfully.
- Do not use full-dataset numbers until you process the full dataset.

## Best Resume Bullet
Built a real-data AI operations intelligence product analyzing 250,000 CFPB complaint records across 12 financial product categories, using NLP, anomaly detection, and evidence retrieval to surface consumer-risk themes, operational bottlenecks, and governed automation opportunities.
